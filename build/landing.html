Two short courses for people who are new to R. The first teaches the R language itself; the second shows you how to read, filter and plot data with the tidyverse. Everything runs in your web browser, in a workbench modelled on RStudio, so there is nothing to install. When you finish a course, you can download a certificate.

Before you start

  • Use a laptop or desktop computer with an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. A wide screen helps, because the instructions sit beside the workbench.
  • Do R basics first: the tidyverse course builds on it.
  • R itself is downloaded into your browser the first time you open a course (about 30 MB), and the tidyverse adds about a minute more the first time you install it.
  • Your ticks, answers, files and name are saved in this browser, on this computer. Use another computer, or a private window, and you start again. Each course has My answers and My files buttons for downloading your work.
  • Each course has an AI assistant you can ask about R, if you have a key for an AI service. Never type personal or patient data into it, or into any other AI tool.

How the courses work

  • Each course is a set of short chapters. Each chapter explains an idea, shows you some R, and then gives you an activity to do in the workbench.
  • The steps of an activity tick themselves when R sees that you have done them, and questions with a Check button are marked straight away.
  • Parts marked If you have time are extras: they don’t count towards your certificate.
  • When every step and question is done, your certificate is unlocked. It shows your name and the date you finished, and you can download it as a PDF.
